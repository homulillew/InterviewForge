# Atomic assertion quality

An AtomicClaim is something the resume actually asserts: implementation, architecture,
choice, outcome, metric, ownership, scale, reliability or optimization. Quote its statement.
“Used Redis Lua; prevented overselling; throughput +40%” yields three assertions, not eight
claims named after interview dimensions. Split explicit ownership without inventing it.

AttackSurface separately owns Dimension, priority, relevance and coverage. Derived topics
must come from the resume, not repository imports or corpus company stereotypes. Preserve
risk factors per assertion. Lexical source relevance is not evidence support and cannot
validate a bold metric. Risk scores are explainable heuristics, not calibrated probabilities.
