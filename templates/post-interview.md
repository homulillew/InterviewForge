# Post Interview Review

State whether this is simulation material analysis or human retest evidence.

1. Resume Claim Coverage — claim IDs and turn counts; mark untested claims.
2. Strongly Defended Claims — supported conclusions and evidence, not inferred mastery.
3. Weakly Defended Claims — which part of the answer remains weak.
4. Unsupported Claims — exact resume assertion and missing evidence.
5. Knowledge Gaps — node + turn/retest anchors.
6. Engineering Gaps — application and integration boundaries.
7. Decision-making Gaps — constraints and alternatives not defended.
8. Failure-mode Gaps — failure paths not addressed.
9. Evaluation Gaps — correctness/performance evidence not supplied.
10. Recommended Next Round — highest-value specific targets.
11. Study Tasks — weakness, root gap, exercise, evidence produced, mastery criterion.
12. Knowledge Graph Update — added/shared nodes and source questions.
